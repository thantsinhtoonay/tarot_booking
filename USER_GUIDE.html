04 · Payment session

Make payment proof easy to review.

The payment session is designed to reduce mistakes and give the astrologer the details needed for a quick decision.

01

Choose the wallet

Select the configured KPay, WavePay, AYA Pay, or CB Pay account. The payment amount is calculated from the selected service and format.

02

Pay and capture proof

Pay only to the selected account, then save a clear screenshot showing the amount and transaction details.

03

Submit for review

Enter the transaction ID, upload the receipt, and reserve. The booking appears as pending payment until the astrologer approves or rejects it.

Payment status meaning: Pending payment means waiting for review; Confirmed means accepted; Rejected means contact the astrologer or submit a corrected booking when appropriate.

07 · Safe daily operation

Protect trust at every step.

Security is part of the customer experience: private bookings, controlled admin actions, and careful payment-proof handling.

Built-in protections

  • Admin routes require an administrator session.
  • Customers can access only their own bookings and profile data.
  • Payment amount is calculated server-side from the selected service and format.
  • Receipt URLs must refer to an uploaded image owned by the customer.
  • Images are checked for supported type, file size, and file signature.
  • State-changing requests are checked against the expected origin.
  • Login and registration attempts are throttled.

Good operating habits

  • Use a unique, long JWT_SECRET in production.
  • Never put passwords, private keys, or payment secrets in public code.
  • Review payment receipts before confirming a booking.
  • Remove unavailable slots before telling customers they are open.
  • Use a production transactional database for multiple server instances.
  • Back up booking data and restrict access to the server storage.

09 · Help center

Frequently asked questions.

Use these answers when onboarding customers or training a new astrologer.

Why can’t I select a date?

The date may be in the past, closed by the astrologer, or outside the currently generated availability window. Choose another open date or contact the astrologer.

Why is a time slot disabled?

The slot may already be booked, temporarily reserved, manually blocked, or outside the current working-hour settings.

Can I book without a Viber number?

No. The Viber number is required for booking confirmation. Telegram and Facebook are optional contact channels.

What happens after payment proof is submitted?

The booking enters Pending payment. The astrologer reviews the transaction and receipt, then confirms or rejects the booking.

Can the astrologer upload any tarot image?

No. Daily tarot selection is intentionally limited to the bundled tarot deck under the public images folder.

How do I change the daily message?

Open the astrologer portal’s Daily message page, choose the effective date, select a bundled card, edit the advice, and publish. An existing message for that date can be updated or deleted.

What should I do if a customer paid but the booking was rejected?

Review the receipt and transaction details, contact the customer through the provided channel, and agree on the next step before creating or confirming another booking.
